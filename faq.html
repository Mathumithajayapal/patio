Orders & Shipping

Materials, Care & Warranty

Teak is incredibly resilient. To maintain its original golden hue, apply a specialized teak sealer or oil annually. Alternatively, if left untreated, it will gracefully weather to a beautiful silvery-grey patina over time. Clean simply using a soft brush and mild soapy water.

Our cushions are highly water-resistant, made from premium performance fabrics designed to repel moisture, resist stains, and prevent mildew. However, they are not 100% waterproof. We strongly recommend covering them or bringing them indoors during heavy rainstorms to prolong their life.

Our industry-leading 10-year warranty covers the structural integrity of frames and any manufacturing defects under normal residential use. It protects against unnatural fading, snapping, and severe weather degradation. Normal wear and tear or misuse is excluded.

Yes, all of our furniture is engineered to withstand freezing temperatures and snow. However, to keep it looking pristine and reduce spring cleaning time, we highly recommend using our custom-fit, breathable furniture covers during extreme winter months.

Our powder-coated aluminum frames are practically maintenance-free. Simply wipe them down periodically with a soft, damp cloth and a mild detergent. Avoid using abrasive sponges, harsh chemicals, or pressure washers, as these can damage the protective finish.

Yes! If you want to refresh the look of your patio or replace an older set, we sell replacement cushions designed to perfectly fit all our standard furniture lines. They are available in over 20 different colors and patterns via our accessories catalog.
