Our Mission

Redefining Outdoor Living

At PatioOasis, we believe that the outdoors should be a seamless extension of your home. Our mission is to blend indoor comfort with outdoor durability, creating spaces where memories are made under the open sky.

We carefully source premium materials to ensure every piece withstands the elements while maintaining its aesthetic appeal. Your backyard is a blank canvas, and we provide the masterpiece.

Collections & Featured Brands

Lounge & Seating

From deep-seating modular sectionals to classic Adirondack chairs, our lounge collections prioritize ergonomic comfort without sacrificing style. We feature top brands like Sunbrella® for fade-resistant fabrics and Polywood® for sustainable, durable frames.

Our Journey

How It All Started

2012

Humble Beginnings

Started in a small garage with a passion for woodworking and a desire to build furniture that could truly withstand the unpredictable coastal weather.

2016

First Retail Store

Opened our flagship location, allowing customers to experience the comfort and craftsmanship of our signature teak and wicker collections in person.

2020

Going National

Expanded operations nationwide, introducing our direct-to-consumer online platform and eco-friendly packaging initiative for all freight deliveries.

2024

Industry Leaders

Recognized as the premier outdoor living brand, introducing our innovative modular aluminum line with lifetime weather resistance guarantee.

Our Promise

A Greener Tomorrow

For every furniture set sold, PatioOasis partners with global reforestation organizations to plant ten trees. We are committed to achieving a carbon-neutral footprint by 2030, ensuring that the beautiful outdoors we celebrate today remains pristine for future generations.
